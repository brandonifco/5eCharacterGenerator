# 2014 Rules Source Inventory

## Purpose

This register identifies the exact official 2014-era rules sources authorized for implementation, testing, interpretation, and compatibility decisions.

A source is not approved merely because it exists or is commonly associated with fifth-edition Dungeons & Dragons.

## Current State

**Status:** Initial core-source inventory approved by the Product Owner on July 27, 2026.

Only the sources recorded below are currently authorized for rules implementation and testing.

Additional official 2014-era sources may be added later through controlled inventory updates.

## Currently Unauthorized Sources

Any official book, supplement, errata document, Sage Advice document, digital rules source, or third-party publication not listed above remains unauthorized for implementation until added through approved change control.

## Source-Control Rules

1. Record the exact source title and relevant printing, revision, or errata state when known.
2. Distinguish internal implementation and testing access from permission to distribute content.
3. Record later official wording that supersedes an earlier source.
4. Do not infer distribution rights from lawful ownership or access.
5. Route conflicting or ambiguous official wording through the rules-interpretation register.
6. Do not implement content or rules from an unlisted source.
