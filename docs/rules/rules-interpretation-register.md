# Rules Interpretation Register

## Purpose

This register controls disputed, ambiguous, or conflicting 2014 rules interpretations.

A rules specialist advises and documents the issue. The Product Owner makes the final decision.

## Status Values

- Open
- Under Specialist Review
- Awaiting Product Owner Decision
- Approved
- Superseded
- Rejected

## Required Record Content

Every interpretation record must identify:

1. The exact official sources and wording at issue.
2. The ambiguity or conflict.
3. The viable interpretations.
4. The mechanical and compatibility effects of each interpretation.
5. The rules specialist's recommendation.
6. The Product Owner's final disposition.
7. Tests, content definitions, or persisted data affected by the decision.
